# 0081 — Ray-traced ambient occlusion in place of SSAO

Status: **KEEP**, off by default (a look) · Branch `fellow/geometry` · Settings
`rendering/fellow/raytracing/ambient_occlusion`, `raytracing/ao_rays` (with `raytracing/enabled`, decision 0080) ·
Code: `servers/rendering/renderer_rd/shaders/effects/fellow_rtao.glsl`, `renderer_rd/effects/fellow_rtao.*`,
`renderer_rd/forward_clustered/render_forward_clustered.*`, `fellow_settings.*`.

## Problem

SSAO (and Godot's is a good one) only knows the depth buffer: what is off screen or hidden behind the first surface
occludes nothing, a thin pole in front of a far wall darkens the wall, the effect fades at the screen's edges and
changes as the camera turns. With the frame's ray tracing scene (0080) the occlusion can be computed from the geometry.

## Change

Where the environment enables SSAO and `raytracing/ambient_occlusion` is on (and the frame has a ray tracing scene, a
single view), three compute passes on the main queue replace SSAO and write the buffer it would (`RB_SCOPE_SSAO /
RB_FINAL`, R8, full resolution), which the colour pass reads unchanged — with the environment's AO light affect and
channel settings:

1. **Trace**: per pixel of the depth pre-pass, `ao_rays` (2) rays over the cosine-weighted hemisphere of the shading
   normal (normal-roughness buffer), up to the environment's SSAO radius, against the geometry the camera sees (instance
   mask bit 0: not shadow-only instances, not alpha-tested surfaces, 0080; back faces culled, first hit). A hit at distance t occludes by 1 − t / radius. The origin is lifted along the geometric
   normal reconstructed from the depth buffer, and rays below that plane are mirrored above it: faceted surfaces with
   smooth normals (the low-poly spheres of City Lab's trees) do not occlude themselves. Directions: a per-pixel offset
   into the R2 low-discrepancy sequence, advanced every frame.
2. **Temporal**: blended with the previous frames' result at the reprojected position (four texels, each kept only at
   the expected depth, 3 %), up to 24 frames.
   **Interleaved** (`raytracing/ao_interleaved`, on): one pixel of each 2 × 2 block traced per frame (all four in four
   frames); the others keep their reprojected history, or where it is lost take the block's new sample, and the history
   counts new samples only (a quarter as many: the same time span).
3. **Denoise**: a depth- and normal-aware 5 × 5 blur with a stride of 2 (wider while few frames are accumulated), then
   the environment's intensity (2: as traced; the occlusion scaled by intensity / 2) and power.

## Verification

lavapipe, City Lab and Crowd Lab captures at 960 × 540, the AO buffer (SSAO debug draw) against SSAO:

* The same contact occlusion at the buildings' bases, under benches and crates, around poles; in addition the far end of
  the street and thin poles in the distance get theirs, and nothing far behind a pole darkens.
* Without the geometric normal, the trees' crowns had blotches (self-occlusion of the facets): gone.
* The street camera, which the benchmark places inside a building (the raster view culls its walls), shows that
  building's contact occlusion on the street around it: correct for the geometry, an artefact of the camera placement.
* Crowd Lab's characters came out black: their tubes were wound inside out (counter-clockwise from outside), so the
  raster pass drew the inside of each tube's far wall, and rays from there hit the near wall's inside. The bench mesh is
  fixed (clockwise from outside, `crowd_lab.gd`): the characters are now lit from outside as intended (they were drawn
  dark before, in stock too), and RTAO darkens the joints slightly. Crowd Lab's images change with the fix; its CPU-side
  results (0076, 0077) do not depend on it.

Cost on the RTX laptop (`m16_f231_rt_ab`, 1080p, in one process against ray tracing with no effect; every pixel traced,
2 rays): City Lab GPU +0.07 ms (RTAO 0.37 ms in place of SSAO's 0.34 ms, plus the scene's build), PBR Lab +0.10 ms,
Sponza +1.55 ms (+25 %: RTAO 2.07 ms against SSAO's 0.91 ms), Bistro exterior +1.33 ms (+33 %). Interleaved tracing
(a quarter of the rays) was added after this measurement: `m16_f233`.

With the milestone's last build (`m16_f246_rt_ab`, `fellow182`, in one process against ray tracing with no effect): GPU
−4 % in City Lab (cheaper than SSAO), +5 % in Sponza (the trace 0.88 ms against SSAO's 1.39 ms; the scene's build
counted), +17 % in Bistro exterior.

## Decision

**KEEP, off by default.**
