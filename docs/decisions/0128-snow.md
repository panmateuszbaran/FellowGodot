## Problem

Milestone 25's `FellowRain3D` makes it rain and wets the scene where the rain falls; milestone 26-27's wind moves
plants, water, gas and particles. Winter had nothing: a project wanting snow paints it into its textures (it cannot
come and go), puts white decals on the ground, or draws its own flakes with particles that fall through roofs and do
not feel the wind.

## Change

`FellowRain3D` gets a **precipitation** mode. With **Snow** the same node does for snow what it does for rain:

* **Flakes** (`max_drops` of them, `rain` scales them) drift down around the camera at `flake_speed` (1.1 m/s), each a
  soft disc of `flake_size` (1.8 cm, ×0.5-1.5) facing the camera, swinging about its path by `flake_flutter`
  (12 cm) at a rate of its own. Rain falls from above the camera; flakes are too slow for that — they live 8 s in a
  box of `area` around the camera, from 3 m below it to `fall_height` above, raised and upwind by half of what they
  fall and drift in a life, fading in and out at its ends. With a `FellowWind3D` they **take the wind where they
  are**, gusts included (the particles' Wind group of decision 0127, `flake_wind_influence` 1.5/s): flakes swirl
  through a gust and hang in a lull. They stop on the scene as seen from above (the shelter heightfield): no flakes
  under roofs. Their shader receives shadows (a flake in a building's shadow is not lit by the sun), lamps light the
  flakes near them. They are drawn in the **opaque pass with hashed alpha**: blended, they wrote no motion vectors,
  and TAA wiped out every flake that moves more than its size in a frame (on lavapipe and on the RTX hardly a flake
  was left with TAA on); opaque, they get the particles' motion vectors (their flutter too: the vertex shader runs
  again with the previous frame's time) and TAA resolves their soft edges.
* **Snow cover** (`snow`, 0-1): the pass that wets materials (decision 0124) covers what faces up with snow, where the
  snow falls — the same shelter heightfield, **eroded by 35 cm** (the lowest of five points): bare under roofs, the
  arcade and the trees' canopies, but not under wires, strings of lights and lamp heads, which had left rows of bare
  dots on Bistro's streets; under a roof's edge the snow reaches 35 cm in, as blown snow does. Thin snow lies in
  patches with ragged edges (the puddles' noise, cells of 2.7 m, 43 cm and 7 cm); deep snow covers everything facing
  up; none on slopes steeper than 60°. Fresh snow: white with a little blue, rough, smooth (it fills the material's
  relief) with gentle undulations of a centimetre or two; `snow_sparkle` glints where a crystal faces the sun (8 mm
  cells, one in 20 turned at random, faded where a cell is smaller than a pixel).
* `snow_follows_precipitation`: the cover grows while it snows (fully in `snowing_time`, 900 s, in full snowfall)
  and melts otherwise (`melting_time`, 1800 s).
* While it snows there are no ripples on puddles or on a `FellowWater3D`, and no splashes.

FellowBench: `--weather_snow=<0-1>` snows in any scene (`--weather_snow_cover`, `--weather_snow_flakes`,
`--weather_snow_area`, `--weather_snow_sparkle`); Rain Lab `--rain=snow` (`--rain_snow_cover`, `--rain_flakes`).

## Verification

* **Rain unchanged.** On lavapipe against the build before: Rain Lab without drops (wet, puddles) 5 images with
  0 pixels differing — the wetting moved into a function of its own and the snow's shelter added — and Water Lab
  5 images with 0 pixels differing. With drops, the splashes differ between two runs of the same build (the order of
  the sub-emitter's particles), so that is not a comparison.
* **A test scene** (lavapipe): what faces up is covered, a 35° slope covered and a 60° one bare, the floor under a
  roof on posts bare, a sphere capped; at `snow` 0.35 the cover lies in patches with ragged edges. A 5 cm wire and a
  40 cm lamp head overhead left a bare strip and a bare square below them before the shelter was eroded, nothing
  after; the bare floor under the roof shrank by 35 cm at its edges.
* **Wind Lab under snow** (lavapipe, 640 × 360): the meadow white with the grass standing out, snow on the leaves that
  face up, bare ground under the trees' canopies.
* **TAA.** The same scene at night with TAA on: blended, almost every flake gone (only the far ones, slow on screen,
  faintly); opaque with hashed alpha, as many as without TAA.
* **Cost** (RTX 5070 Ti Laptop, 1080p, Rain Lab, 2 runs each, `m28_f289`): snowing on a covered street 2.300 ms of GPU
  time against 1.926 ms without `FellowRain3D` (+0.37 ms: the depth pre-pass +0.10, the opaque pass +0.10 — the
  60000 flakes and the cover —, the flakes' update +0.07); raining 2.266 ms (+0.34). With the flakes blended
  (`m28_f288`) snowing had cost +0.24 ms: the flakes in the pre-pass and the opaque pass are the 0.13 ms difference.
* **Bistro** (RTX, `m28_f288`/`f289` showcase): the streets, the café's awnings, its planters and the pots covered,
  the terrace under the awnings bare, the snow at night lit by the lamps, after sunset under a low sun.

## Decision

**KEEP.** Nothing changes until a FellowRain3D snows or has a snow cover.
