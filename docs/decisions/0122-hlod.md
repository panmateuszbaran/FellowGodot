# 0122 — Automatic HLOD: far static meshes merged into proxies

Status: **KEEP** (a new node) · Branch `fellow/geometry` · Node `FellowHLOD3D` (module `fellow_world`, new feature) · Code:
`modules/fellow_world/fellow_hlod_3d.{h,cpp}`, `servers/rendering/renderer_scene_cull.{h,cpp}` (per-cascade shadows,
`RenderingServer::fellow_instance_set_hlod`) · Bench: City Lab `--city_hlod=<distance>`.

## Problem

A city or a level is thousands of separate static meshes. Beyond a few hundred metres each is a handful of pixels, but
each still costs its culling, render list element, instance data and draw (or a share of an instanced one) in the
camera's passes and in every shadow cascade it meets. Engines replace far groups of static meshes by merged proxies
(Unreal's HLOD, Unity's LOD groups with combined meshes). Godot has the mechanism — visibility ranges and visibility
parents — but the proxies have to be modelled and wired by hand.

## Change

`FellowHLOD3D` does it at run time for the meshes below it.

**Members.** Every `MeshInstance3D` below the node that nothing makes per-instance: no skin or blend shapes, no overlay
or transparency, no visibility range or parent of its own, not shadows-only, not top-level, visible, not in the group
`fellow_hlod_exclude`, and no other visual instance (light, particles, decal) below it — a visibility parent passes to
the children that have none, so a light under a merged lamp would vanish with it. Surfaces with bone weights or custom
arrays leave their mesh out.

**Instanced proxies** (`instancing`, with MultiMesh culling on; added after measuring the merged ones alone, see
*First version* below). A mesh repeated at least `instance_threshold` times (the same mesh and material override, the
same cast shadow setting, layers and GI mode), with materials the GPU can cull one by one (`BaseMaterial3D` without
billboard, grow, point or fixed size; no surface override materials, extra cull margin or custom box; not mirrored),
becomes one MultiMesh per kind for the whole node:

* In the camera's passes the renderer culls its instances on the GPU (0034) and also leaves out every instance closer
  than `distance` (by its box's center); each member's own visibility range ends at `distance`, judged by the scene
  cull on the same point. The same mesh, material and transform either way, so the switch is per object, does not
  show and needs no hysteresis. Such a job must cull: it is never left whole (any instance count, not under the
  small-set rule or autotune).
* Stock MultiMeshes transform normals by the instance's basis, the nodes they stand in for by its inverse transpose
  (`INSTANCE_FLAGS_NON_UNIFORM_SCALE`): with a non-uniform scale the shading differed (City Lab's prisms and spheres,
  29.7 dB in the first try). An HLOD proxy's instances use the inverse transpose (a flag of the proxy's instance data,
  `INSTANCE_FLAGS_FELLOW_INSTANCE_NORMALS`; stock MultiMeshes are unchanged).
* In shadow passes the scene cull tests nodes also against the light culler's planes (casters whose shadow cannot reach
  what the camera sees); the GPU test of a proxy's instances gets the same planes (`FellowShadowCacheOp.caster_planes`),
  so a far cascade draws the same casters as without the node.
* Any number of far objects costs one draw per kind (and its LOD buckets with 0035) instead of a share of the
  per-object work of each.

**Merged proxies** for the rest (meshes used few times, or with other materials). Members are grouped by the cell of a
grid (`cell_size`, the node's space) holding their box's center and by their per-instance settings. Per cell with at
least `min_meshes` members, their triangle surfaces are merged per material and per set of vertex attributes
(transforms applied: positions; normals by the inverse transpose; tangents, their sign flipped and the triangles turned
over under a mirroring transform), with detail levels generated as the mesh importer does (`generate_lods`; Godot's mesh
LOD then draws far cells coarser). An internal `MeshInstance3D` draws each proxy from `distance` on (its visibility
range's begin, `margin` as hysteresis); each member gets its proxy as visibility parent: a cell switches at once.

**Impostors** (`impostor_distance` > 0; added after the RTX measurement of the above). Far trees are where triangles
go: a mesh of at least `impostor_min_triangles` (256) triangles that has an instanced proxy, and every
`MultiMeshInstance3D` below the node whose instances the GPU can cull (its materials as above, no range, margin or
custom box of its own), gets an octahedral impostor baked at run time (`FellowImpostorBaker`, an internal node):

* **Bake.** The mesh alone in a world of its own, two viewports of `impostor_resolution` pixels sharing it — the
  unshaded debug draw (albedo and coverage, alpha-scissored materials cut as drawn) and the normal-buffer debug draw
  (the view's normals) — and an orthographic camera per view: `impostor_frames` x `impostor_frames` views over the
  sphere (octahedral mapping, y up), one view per frame (about 70 frames for 8 x 8). The normals are turned from each
  view's space into the mesh's; colours and normals are spread 8 texels into the uncovered texels of each view (so the
  mipmaps do not darken the edges); both atlases get mipmaps. `FELLOW_IMPOSTOR_DUMP=<dir>` saves them.
* **Draw.** A quad mesh whose box is the bounding sphere's (centred where the mesh's box is: the per-instance distance
  tests judge the same point), a shader that turns it towards the camera (towards the light in an orthographic shadow
  pass) and blends the four views nearest to the view direction (each at the quad point's projection in that view),
  alpha-scissored, its normal from the normal atlas. It moves vertices but never out of its box, so the GPU still
  culls its instances (`fellow_instance_set_instance_range(..., vertices_bounded)`).
* **Hand-over.** The instanced proxy (or the MultiMesh itself) draws its instances up to `impostor_distance`, the
  impostors from it on, per instance; in the shadows per cascade (a proxy may now stand in only up to a distance:
  `fellow_instance_set_hlod(..., end_distance)`). Until a bake is done the meshes are drawn.

**Shadows.** With stock's visibility dependencies every shadow pass draws what the camera's distance selects, and the
directional shadow cache (0004) cannot keep any of it (what it holds must not depend on where the camera is). The
renderer is therefore told which instances are proxies and members (`RenderingServer::fellow_instance_set_hlod`, a flag
per instance) and decides per cascade: a cascade starting beyond `distance` draws the proxies, the others the members,
whatever the camera's distance to them — camera-independent, so the cache keeps them. Instanced proxies always; merged
ones with `SHADOW_MODE_PER_CASCADE` (default; `SHADOW_MODE_FOLLOW_CAMERA`: stock's rule). Positional shadows draw the
members (a proxy only stands in for far objects).

## First version: everything merged per cell

City Lab with `--city_hlod=100` (cells of 44 m, 4 x 4 lots; 18 814 meshes into 196 proxies of 2 301 surfaces, 600 k
triangles; built in 1.0 s with detail levels, 0.34 s without), `fellow` at `c39f6d2` + this change, lavapipe:

| captures (960 x 540) vs the city without the node | aerial | roofs | street |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| merged, detail levels | 47.2 dB | 50.9 dB | 69.2 dB |
| merged, no detail levels | 68.7 dB | 70.9 dB | 94.9 dB |
| merged, detail levels, shadows following the camera | 46.8 dB | 50.4 dB | 69.2 dB |

Without detail levels the proxies draw what the meshes drew (baked transforms round differently: 16 levels at most).
The detail levels are what changes: the tree crowns' and lamps' spheres lose rings at a pixel or less each, over
thousands of them. That is the project's mesh LOD trade-off (`mesh_lod_threshold`), as for 0035.

Perf (480 x 270, lavapipe, one run; per fixed camera, 40 frames):

| | objects | draws | shadow draws | primitives | lavapipe GPU | render thread |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| aerial: meshes | 19 636 | 201 | 158 | 609 k | 291 ms | 6.45 ms |
| aerial: merged proxies | 3 003 | 1 540 | 132 | 191 k | 245 ms | 7.61 ms |
| street: meshes | 16 294 | 337 | 308 | 504 k | 240 ms | 5.24 ms |
| street: merged proxies | 3 961 | 1 133 | 223 | 183 k | 201 ms | 6.00 ms |

Fewer objects and a third of the triangles, but **more draws and more render-thread time**: City Lab's 18 800 meshes
are a dozen meshes and materials, which Godot already draws instanced (a draw per mesh and material for every node
that has them); a merged proxy is a mesh of its own, a draw per cell and material. Merging pays where meshes are
unique; for repeated ones it undoes the instancing. Hence the instanced proxies.

## Final version: instanced proxies

City Lab with `--city_hlod=100`: all 18 814 meshes become members of 14 instanced proxies (14 kinds of mesh and
material; built in 44 ms), lavapipe:

* **Image:** captures at 960 x 540 against the city without the node: 0 differing pixels in all three (with
  `multimesh_instance_lod` too). The first try differed (29.7 dB aerial) — the MultiMesh normals under non-uniform
  scales, fixed as above.
* **Off:** without the node, and with every new path idle, bit for bit the image of `c39f6d2` (City Lab and Foliage
  Lab one MultiMesh per type, 0 differing pixels).

Perf, 480 x 270, per fixed camera (40 frames), the mean of two runs (ABAB):

| camera | objects | draws | shadow draws | render thread | lavapipe GPU |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| aerial | 19 636 → 1 745 | 201 → 183 | 158 → 129 | 7.19 → 4.16 ms (−42 %) | 304.1 → 286.0 ms (−6 %) |
| street | 16 294 → 3 241 | 337 → 328 | 308 → 279 | 5.63 → 4.18 ms (−26 %) | 256.4 → 249.0 ms (−3 %) |
| roofs | 12 235 → 2 342 | 310 → 284 | 281 → 235 | 4.72 → 3.64 ms (−23 %) | 237.0 → 236.6 ms (0 %) |

An earlier session (the same build without the caster planes) gave −48 / −23 / −21 % on the render thread, with
the GPU 6-20 % slower: the GPU pass timestamps put it all in the directional shadows (aerial 49 → 77 ms), the far
cascade drawing the proxies' instances that the light culler would have left out as nodes. With the caster planes the
shadows cost what they cost without the node (aerial 50.9 → 51.2 ms, street 59.4 → 57.5, roofs 44.1 → 46.4).

With contribution culling as well (0121, 1 px of 480): render thread 3.76 / 3.95 / 3.79 ms.

The scene cull still visits every member (its range rejects it), which is what is left of the far objects' CPU
cost: the render lists, instance data and shadow lists no longer see them.

## Decision

**KEEP** — a new node, nothing changes without it. Instanced proxies are the default and give the same image; merged
proxies are for unique meshes (their generated detail levels change far silhouettes as the project's mesh LOD
allows).
