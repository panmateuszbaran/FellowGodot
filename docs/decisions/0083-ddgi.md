## Problem

What makes an engine's image read as "lit" rather than "shaded" is mostly indirect light: a sunlit street brightening
the shaded walls above it, coloured surfaces tinting their neighbours, interiors lit by what comes through the openings.
Stock Godot's options are baked lightmaps, VoxelGI (baked voxels, static) and SDFGI (signed distance cascades: dynamic,
but blurry and leaky, costly to update). Unreal's Lumen is the reference look. With the frame's ray tracing scene (0080)
the classic real-time answer is DDGI (Majercik et al., "Dynamic Diffuse Global Illumination with Ray-Traced Irradiance
Fields", JCGT 2019): a grid of probes that trace rays every frame and store irradiance with a visibility term.

## Change

* **The volume.** `gi_probes_xz` × `gi_probes_y` × `gi_probes_xz` probes (24 × 8 × 24 by default), `gi_probe_spacing` apart
  (2 m: a 48 × 16 × 48 m volume), centred on the camera's cell. It scrolls: a cell's probe lives in slot `cell mod counts`,
  so moving the camera keeps every probe still in the volume; a slot that receives a new cell starts over (a buffer
  keeps each slot's cell).
* **Update** (every frame, after the ray tracing scene is built, three compute passes):
  1. **Trace**: `gi_rays` rays per probe, spherical Fibonacci directions with a random rotation per frame (deterministic
     in the frame number), against the scene's camera-visible geometry (instance mask bit 0), no culling. A back face (of
     a single-sided material) is recorded as a negative distance; a back face of a double-sided one is lit as a surface but
     its distance is stored negative too, so that it counts towards finding a probe inside a mesh modelled double-sided
     (the instances' facing is Godot's whatever their culling: `FLIP_FACING` with `CULL_DISABLE`). Sponza's walls and
     columns had left probes inside them active and black: arcades and a column came out black on the RTX (`m16_f235`). A hit is lit like a diffuse surface in the scene
     shader's units: its diffuse albedo (BaseMaterial3D's `albedo`, linear, × (1 − `metallic`)) × (the sun: the first
     directional light's colour and energy × N·L / π, the scene shader's Lambert, × a shadow ray, front faces culled as in
     0082; + the volume's irradiance at the hit from the previous
     frame — further bounces, × 0.95 —, or half the ambient light outside the volume) + its emission (`emission` ×
     `emission_energy` × the camera's exposure). The hit's normal is the triangle's, from its vertex positions
     (`VK_KHR_ray_tracing_position_fetch`, now requested by the driver; acceleration structures built with
     `ALLOW_DATA_ACCESS`; the triangle taken to world space first, then its cross product: the first version inverted
     the instance transform, singular for a compressed flat mesh — its box, and so the transform's scale, is 0 thick —, and
     the NaN spread through the probes' bounces: Sponza's gallery and a column came out black on the RTX, `m16_f238`; a
     degenerate triangle takes the reversed ray, and a NaN or infinite radiance is never stored), or the reversed ray
     direction without the extension. A miss sees the environment's ambient
     light in that direction (the scene shader's ambient: its colour and energy mixed with the sky's radiance by the sky
     contribution, the radiance taken at its most blurred level).
  2. **Distance**: per probe, a 16 × 16 octahedral tile (one-texel border) of the mean and mean square of the hit distances
     around each direction (cos⁵⁰ weights), blended with `gi_hysteresis`.
  3. **Irradiance**: per probe, an 8 × 8 octahedral tile of the cosine-weighted mean radiance (the "ambient light" the
     scene shader multiplies by albedo), blended with `gi_hysteresis`; alpha 0 marks a probe inside geometry (a quarter
     of its rays or more saw back faces), skipped by the lookups.
* **Interleaved updates** (`gi_update_interval`, 4): a frame updates the probes whose index is the frame modulo the
  interval (a quarter of them by default), with the hysteresis raised to the interval's power (the same response time);
  a probe that the volume moved to a new cell is marked inactive in the frames before its update, so that no lookup
  reads the old cell's light. All probes start inactive (the atlas cleared at creation).
* **Texture albedo at hits.** The rays have no texture coordinates; a hit's albedo was its material's `albedo` alone —
  white for most imported materials, whose colour is in the texture (Sponza, Bistro): every surface reflected all the
  light it received, in grey. The albedo is now multiplied by the mean colour of the material's `texture_albedo`: on
  first sight of a texture a compute pass (`fellow_rt_albedo.glsl`, one workgroup per texture) averages 8 × 8 samples of
  its mip level of about 8 × 8 texels (sRGB view: linear values, weighed by alpha: cut-out texels do not count) into a
  buffer that is read back asynchronously (no stall; up to 4 096 textures); the materials and surfaces computed before
  it arrives are refreshed then. Counter `raytracing_albedo_textures`. Reflections (0084) see the same colours.
* **Probe relocation** (`gi_probe_relocation`, on; after RTXGI): each update moves the probe from its cell's position by
  what its rays saw — through the nearest back face, a little beyond, when it is inside geometry (a quarter of its rays
  or more saw back faces), away from the nearest surface when that is closer than a fifth of the spacing (along the
  most open direction when that leads away from it) — within 0.45 spacings of the cell (a move beyond is not made). The
  offset lives in a strip of the distance atlas below the tiles (two texels per probe), so the lookups (scene shader,
  screen pass, reflections) read it without a new binding; a slot that receives a new cell starts at the cell's
  position.
* **A coarser cascade** (`gi_cascades`, 2): a second volume with the same probe counts at four times the spacing (8 m:
  192 × 64 × 192 m by default) around the camera, updated at a quarter of the rate (a probe every 16 frames, the
  hysteresis raised accordingly) with half the rays, its bounces from itself (at half the rate and every ray it had cost
  City Lab 0.67 ms on the RTX, as much as the fine volume, `m16_f244`). The fine volume's screen pass, its bounces and the reflections' hits read
  it where the fine volume does not cover the point (beyond it, or where its probes are all hidden from the point):
  irradiance = (fine × c₀ + coarse × (1 − c₀) × c₁) / coverage, coverage c₀ + (1 − c₀) × c₁; what neither covers keeps the
  environment's ambient light.
* **Transparent surfaces** read the screen pass's texture too (where it exists): the irradiance of the opaque surface
  behind them, at its normal — an approximation, as Unreal lights translucency from a coarser volume than its opaque
  surfaces. Looking the probes up in the transparent shaders had cost Sponza's transparent pass 0.76-0.85 ms on the RTX
  (`m16_f243`-`m16_f245`), with the visibility test and without it alike (the lookup's code in shaders already heavy).
  Without the screen pass (MSAA, several views, `debug/raytracing_gi_in_shader`) they look the fine volume up.
* **Path-traced reference** (`debug/raytracing_gi_reference` = bounces, 0 off): in place of the screen pass, a path per
  pixel from its position in the depth buffer (cosine-weighted, the sun sampled with a shadow ray at every hit, up to n
  bounces, misses seeing the environment's ambient light as the probes' rays do), accumulated in an RGBA32F texture
  while the camera stands still (restarted when it moves or the setting changes). It is the irradiance the probes
  approximate — the scene shader multiplies it by the surfaces' own albedo, without the sky leak —, for comparing DDGI
  against (with SSAO/RTAO off: the paths carry their own occlusion). Capture with `--settle=N` for N paths per pixel.
* **Surface data.** Each instance of the ray tracing scene now carries, as its custom index, the index of its surface's
  data (albedo, double-sidedness, emission; cached per material, read again every 120 frames), uploaded per frame.
* **Lookup** (scene shader, `fellow_ddgi_inc.glsl`): the colour passes (opaque, motion, transparent: the volume is in
  world space) are compiled with a new specialization constant (`fellow_ddgi`, `packed_2` bit 2) and read the volume
  (bindings 44-46 of the render pass set) in place of the environment's ambient light: the eight probes around the point
  (offset along the normal and towards the eye by 0.2 and 0.16 spacings), trilinear weights × a smooth backface weight ×
  Chebyshev's visibility bound from the distance tile (crushed below 0.2: less leaking through thin walls), the irradiance
  interpolated in square-root space. Where every active probe around the point is hidden from it, the normalized sum
  would be the least hidden probe's light — often a probe in a sealed void or behind a wall, black: the coverage is
  scaled by how visible the probes are (an unoccluded probe weighs at least 0.2 of its trilinear weight), so such points
  take the ambient light instead. Where the volume ends the result is blended with the environment's ambient light by
  the share of the trilinear weights inside it. Reflection probes, lightmapped surfaces and custom `IRRADIANCE` keep theirs;
  SSAO/RTAO, SSIL and specular reflections apply as before.
* **Sky leak** (`gi_sky_leak`, 0.1): a share of the environment's ambient light is added to the volume's, as Unreal's
  Lumen does ("skylight leaking"). DDGI is physically darker than Godot's ambient light, which is never occluded: in
  Sponza on the RTX the probes in the courtyard held about 13 % of the light of the probes outside the building, those in
  the galleries behind the curtains under 1 % (`m16_f240`, the probes dumped with `debug/raytracing_gi_dump_every`), and
  the galleries came out black — light that cloth would let through and further bounces the volume does not carry.
* **Screen pass** (single view, no MSAA): the lookup above runs once per pixel of the depth pre-pass in a compute pass,
  from the depth and the normal-roughness buffer (written for it when nothing else asks), into an RGBA16F texture of the
  irradiance and its coverage; the opaque and motion colour passes (another specialization constant,
  `fellow_ddgi_screen`, `packed_2` bit 4) read one texel of it (binding 48) instead of looking eight probes up: on the
  RTX the lookup had cost Sponza's opaque pass 1.28 ms. Transparent passes look the probes up as before. The normal is
  the buffer's 8-bit one: City Lab's street 4 836 of 129 600 pixels differ from the lookup in the shader by at most 5
  (`debug/raytracing_gi_in_shader` restores it for comparisons).

## Verification

lavapipe, City Lab captures (16 × 6 × 16 probes, 64 rays, 2 m), against the defaults:

* Off, and ray tracing on without it: the image is unchanged (the milestone 16 verification run).
* On, the street camera: the shaded walls take the colour of the sunlit street and of the walls facing them (the green
  building's shadow side green-lit from below), the shadowed street is lit by the facades around it and darkens where
  little sees the sky, the trees' crowns are lit from below by the street. The aerial and roof cameras are above the
  volume (16 m high around a camera 30-70 m up): unchanged, the blend to the ambient light hides the volume's edge.

The first version lit the hits with the sun π times too bright (the scene shader's 1 / π left out) and metals as
diffuse surfaces; found with 0084's reflections (a chrome sphere reflecting the ground brighter than the ground), fixed
in both.

Cost on the RTX laptop (`m16_f231_rt_ab`, 1080p, in one process against ray tracing with no effect; 24 × 8 × 24 probes,
128 rays, every probe every frame, the lookup in the shader): GPU +1.34 ms in City Lab (+78 %; the update 1.01 ms),
+1.86 ms in PBR Lab, +3.69 ms in Sponza (the update 1.52 ms, the opaque pass +1.28 ms), +2.41 ms in Bistro exterior. The
interleaved updates and the screen pass were added after this measurement (`m16_f233`). With an update interval of 4 a
still image differs from updating every probe every frame by at most 18 levels (City Lab's street, 56.8 dB).

With the milestone's last build (`m16_f246_rt_ab`, `fellow182`: two cascades, texture albedo, relocation, the screen
pass also for transparent surfaces; in one process against ray tracing with no effect): GPU +57 % in City Lab (the
update and screen pass 1.07 ms), +22 % in Sponza (1.46 ms), +44 % in Bistro exterior (1.61 ms).

## Decision

**KEEP, off by default.**
