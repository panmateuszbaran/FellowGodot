# 0125 — Wind: grass, bushes and trees bending, swaying and fluttering with the wind and its gusts

Status: **KEEP** (a new node and a material option; without them nothing of it runs) · Branch `fellow/geometry` ·
Node `FellowWind3D` (module `fellow_fluid`), `BaseMaterial3D`'s Wind group, the spatial render mode `fellow_wind`
with the vertex built-ins `WIND_HEIGHT`, `WIND_BEND`, `WIND_FLUTTER`, `WIND_SHEEN` · Code:
`modules/fellow_fluid/fellow_wind_3d.{h,cpp}` (the node, the gusts' noise), the wind's state in
`servers/rendering/renderer_rd/effects/fellow_weather.{h,cpp}` (the weather's uniform buffer, this frame's and the
previous one's wind; the gusts' texture at binding 52), `fellow_wind_offset` and its calls in
`shaders/forward_clustered/scene_forward_clustered.glsl`, the render mode and built-ins in `servers/rendering/shader_types.cpp`
and the three renderers' compilers, `scene/resources/material.{h,cpp}`; the rain's drops in `fellow_rain_3d.cpp`;
bench scene Wind Lab (`--scene=wind`).

## Problem

The user asked (2026-10-29), after rain (0124), for wind in the forest. Stock Godot has no wind: every project sways
its vegetation in a vertex shader of its own — a sine of `TIME` with a phase per plant, the whole plant moving as one
(Foliage Lab's `--foliage_wind=1`, 0036, is such a shader). Nothing makes gusts roll over a meadow, a tree bend more
than its leaves flutter, or the rain lean with the same wind.

## Change

`FellowWind3D` is the scene's wind (one active at a time): blowing along the node's forward axis on the ground plane
at `speed` (6 m/s); **gusts** are a tiling noise (256², two octaves of value noise, eight cells a side, stretched
twofold around the middle) laid along the wind (its cells `gust_size` across, twice as long along it) and travelling with it
at the mean speed; the speed at a point is `speed · (1 + gustiness · clamp(3 (g − ½), −1, 1))`. The node hands the
renderer its state once per frame (direction, speed, gustiness, how far the gusts have travelled, their size, the
flutter's scale, the noise's texture); the renderer keeps the previous frame's for the motion vectors.
`get_velocity_at()` samples the same field on the CPU.

**A plant in the wind** (`fellow_wind_offset`, the vertex shader, after the material's `vertex()`): each mesh instance
(a MultiMesh's instance or a node) is a plant standing at its origin, `WIND_HEIGHT` tall in its mesh (from y = 0). With
h the height along it (0 to 1) and its force f = (v / 10 m/s)² from the speed at its origin:

* it **bends** along the wind: the top by `b = WIND_BEND · f` of the height, softly limited to ¾ (0.75 b / (0.75 + b)),
  the displacement growing with h² (a cantilever's shape), the vertices lowered to keep the stem's length;
* it **sways** about that: ±25 % along the wind and 15 % across, at a natural frequency of 1/√(height in m) Hz (a 10 m
  tree at 0.3 Hz, a blade of grass at 1.4 Hz), a phase per plant; the gusts passing change the force from plant to
  plant and in time, so they roll over a meadow in waves;
* **branches** — vertices away from the stem (a tree's crown, a clump's blades) — sway at their own phases (coherent
  over a branch), by 3 % of the height plus 8 % per unit of force at the radius;
* **leaves flutter** (`WIND_FLUTTER`, `FellowWind3D.flutter`): along their normals at 4-7 Hz, a phase per vertex;
* the **normal turns** with the stem's tilt (a small rotation about up × tilt), so a bent meadow is lit differently;
* a **sheen** (`WIND_SHEEN`): the albedo multiplied by 1 + sheen · bend (a varying), the lighter, glossier side of bent
  grass.

The displacement is added in view space after the model-view transform (no inverse of the model matrix per vertex).
The wind has its own clock (`time`, `time_auto`: the sway's phase and the gusts' travel, so a bench or a cutscene sets
the same pose for the same time). The previous frame's wind and clock (the vertex shader runs again for the motion
vectors, with the previous frame's scene data) give the motion vectors: TAA and motion blur see the motion. Shadow passes run the same vertex shader. A
`fellow_wind` material counts as one that writes `VERTEX` (MultiMesh culling and the shadow cache treat it as moving).
Not in SDFGI's voxelization, not with `skip_vertex_transform`; the Mobile and Compatibility renderers compile the
built-ins but do not move the vertices.

**Culling and shadows.** A `fellow_wind` material counts as one that moves vertices, but its displacement is bounded:
at a height y of the mesh, at most 0.95 y across (the bend and the branches), 0.37 y down and 0.08 y up, the flutter
at most 0.24 m (clamped). MultiMesh culling (0034) grows each instance's box by that bound instead of drawing a wind
MultiMesh whole (no cull margin needed). The directional shadow cache (0004) keeps such casters dynamic in the near
cascades and **caches them from cascade `shadows/wind_cached_from_cascade` on** (2: the third and fourth of four; -1
never): a far cascade keeps the pose of its last build (a tree 25 m away whose shadow does not sway).

**Materials.** A `BaseMaterial3D` gets a Wind group (`wind`, `wind_height`, `wind_bend`, `wind_flutter`,
`wind_sheen`): its shader adds the render mode and sets the built-ins from uniforms. A `ShaderMaterial` writes
`render_mode fellow_wind;` and sets the built-ins in its `vertex()`. The user varyings of Forward+ shaders start one
location later (the sheen's varying at 15).

**The rain** (`FellowRain3D.wind_from_scene`, on): its drops are carried by the scene's wind at the camera, with the
gust there (updated when it changes by more than 0.25 m/s).

## Results

**Exactness.** Without a FellowWind3D and without wind materials: City Lab, Water Lab, Rain Lab `--rain=off` and
Foliage Lab with its TIME-based sway (`--foliage_wind=1`) bit for bit the images of `d8f2909` (the user varyings one
location later and the weather's uniform buffer larger change no pixel).

**Cost on lavapipe** (the user asked not to load the laptop's GPU for now: no RTX measurement yet). Wind Lab with one
grass clump per m² (`--wind_grass=1`; lavapipe's vertex work is slow), 480 x 270, 2 passes of 60 frames, 20 frames per
camera, GPU time of the viewport in ms (one run each; lavapipe's spread about 5 %):

| Wind Lab | flythrough | meadow | edge | forest | aerial |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| still (`--wind=off`) | 840 | 587 | 216 | 180 | 862 |
| the sway projects write (`--wind=shader`: TIME, no gusts) | 2079 | 1974 | 1484 | 1000 | 1505 |
| FellowWind3D, drawn whole, shadows never cached | 2304 | 2151 | 1694 | 1165 | 1710 |
| culled with the wind's bound | 2079 | 1733 | 1067 | 862 | 1639 |
| the same (`shadows/wind_cached_from_cascade` = -1) | 1985 | 1875 | 1103 | 865 | 1541 |
| far cascades cached from the fourth (= 3) | 1724 | 1609 | 913 | 764 | 1231 |
| **from the third (= 2, the default)** | **1303** | **1067** | **623** | **555** | **1016** |
| no sun shadows: still | 670 | 587 | 198 | 172 | 777 |
| no sun shadows: FellowWind3D | 787 | 620 | 195 | 186 | 967 |

The wind's cost is its shadows: a caster whose vertices move cannot be kept in the shadow cache (0004), so every
cascade draws the meadow and the forest again each frame. Without sun shadows the vertex work costs 0-25 %. Culling
with the bound instead of drawing wind MultiMeshes whole: −10 % (flythrough), −19 to −37 % on the ground; caching the
far cascades from the third: −34 to −43 % more (from the fourth: −12 to −20 %). In the end FellowWind3D costs 32-58 %
less than the TIME-based shaders projects write for the same scene (those count as moving vertices: drawn whole, never
cached) and 55 % more than the still scene (flythrough).
