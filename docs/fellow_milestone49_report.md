# FellowEngine — milestone 49 report (platforms)

Status: **checked on the cloud's CPU, in containers and emulators, and the three renderers on the RTX** (2026-11-05;
no other hardware). Branch `fellow/geometry`. Decision record `docs/decisions/0150-platform-checks.md`.

## 0. Result

FellowEngine had run on one Windows laptop with an NVIDIA GPU, Vulkan and Forward+. Without other machines, milestone
49 ran it the ways Godot's other platforms would: every bench lab with Godot's three renderers (Forward+ and Mobile on
lavapipe, Compatibility on llvmpipe's OpenGL), eight labs on **nine simulated GPUs** — Android's 2022 and 2025
baselines, Intel UHD 620 and Arc A770, GTX 1060, RTX 4070, RX 580, RX 7800 XT, Apple M1 through MoltenVK — made by a
new diagnostic in the Vulkan driver (`FELLOW_VULKAN_PROFILE`), the **Windows** template built with MinGW in a container
and run under Wine, and the **Linux arm64** template cross-built and run under qemu. Nothing crashes any more and the
images are Linux's; on the way the checks found **nine faults** and they are fixed, the worst a crash on every GPU
without buffer device addresses (Android's, older desktop ones) and the Mobile renderer losing positional lights (its
Lights Lab 4 × darker than stock's).

What the simulated devices say: on **Android's 2025 baseline and every desktop GPU profile FellowEngine asks for
nothing the device lacks** (the eight labs, no error, no finding); on Apple's M1 through MoltenVK only stock's scene
shader's 18 samplers; on Vulkan's minimums (Android's 2022 baseline) stock's Mobile renderer does not fit either.
Ray tracing turns itself off on devices without ray queries, and the image is the one without it exactly. The Windows
build under Wine renders the PBR Lab as Linux does (150.6 / 40.8 luma mean / spread), on Vulkan and on its OpenGL
fallback; the arm64 and clang builds give Linux's results (Matching Lab's statistics, the labs' images). On the RTX,
the Mobile and Compatibility renderers give stock Godot's images in every lab both run (no pixel more than 1 of 255
apart), with FellowEngine's 17 labs running without an error in all three.

## 1. Development

* **`FELLOW_VULKAN_PROFILE`** (`drivers/vulkan/fellow_vulkan_profile.{h,cpp}`, `fellow_vulkan_profile_data.h`
  generated by `benchmarks/tools/platforms/make_vulkan_profiles.py` from the Khronos Vulkan-Profiles data): the
  device's extensions, features, limits, subgroup operations and compressed formats reduced to another device's, and
  every shader checked against it (SPIR-V capabilities, workgroup sizes, shared memory, descriptors a stage and a
  layout, push constants, uniform buffer ranges), reported when a pipeline is made from it.
* **`benchmarks/tools/platforms/`**: `renderer_matrix.sh` (the labs on the three renderers, image statistics, errors,
  portability findings), `profile_matrix.sh` (the labs on the simulated devices), `platforms.sh` and four
  Dockerfiles (cross-compilers, Wine, an arm64 runtime with Ubuntu's arm64 Mesa; Android's NDK and Emscripten prepared,
  not downloaded).
* **Fixes**: index buffers' uninitialized creation flags (a crash without buffer device addresses); the Mobile
  renderer's light loop (FellowEngine's early outs dropped the following lights); stock's DirectInput hook calling
  `VirtualProtect()` with a null pointer (a crash under Wine, a call that fails on Windows); varyings passed from
  `fragment()` to `light()` counted as interpolants (stock), and the water's two in one `vec4`; gas volumes simulated
  dense where the sparse kernels' 11 storage images a stage do not fit, their trace pass reading textures through one
  sampler instead of 27; the glow's 16 × 16 tiles only where 256 invocations a workgroup run; MultiMesh culling's
  buffer leaked at exit; MinGW's build (`windows.h`'s `near`/`far`, shadowed locals, `swprintf_s`); warnings instead
  of errors from FellowEngine's nodes in the Compatibility renderer, and the water shader compiling there.

## 4. Limits

* A simulated device is lavapipe with features taken away: no other GPU's performance, driver bugs, memory, or
  subgroup size (8 lanes on lavapipe, 32 on NVIDIA, 32 or 64 on AMD, 8-32 on Intel); what lavapipe lacks (cooperative
  matrices) is missing under every profile.
* Android's and the web's templates are not built (the NDK and Emscripten are downloads not made); macOS and iOS need
  Apple's SDK; consoles a third-party port. Godot's Metal and Direct3D 12 drivers carry none of FellowEngine's driver
  work.
* The Mobile renderer simulates FellowGasVolume3D but does not draw it; the Compatibility renderer runs none of the
  RenderingDevice nodes (gas, burning, water's simulation, shallow water, rain's ripples).
* Android's 2022 baseline (Vulkan's minimums) is beyond stock Godot's Mobile renderer too (18 samplers, 27-31
  sampled images and 10 storage buffers a stage); FellowEngine's rain and water compute passes also exceed it (256
  invocations, 5 storage images); the 2025 baseline runs everything.
